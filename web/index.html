Mechanistic Biophysics

The Physics That Makes It Move

Biological locomotion is not an arbitrary optimization problem. It emerges from a closed mechanical loop: membrane potentials translate into discrete axonal delays, neuromuscular junction tensions deform an elastic hydrostatic cylinder, and viscous fluid drag produces forward momentum.

Synaptic Delays & Reciprocal Cross-Inhibition

Objective: Test whether non-zero axonal conduction delays and GABAergic reciprocal inhibition prevent runaway population seizures.

Result: Implementing a 1.5 ms discrete ring buffer delay line eliminated the 45.5 Hz seizure observed in prior uncalibrated models. B-type motor neurons excite DD/VD motor neurons, hyperpolarizing the opposing quadrant and establishing clean, stable 1.62 Hz anti-phase undulation.

Evidence: connecto/engine/snn.py · 200/200 trials verified stable
STAGE 03

Closed-Loop Hydrodynamic Locomotion

PASSED · 0.52 MM/S CRAWL

Objective: Couple the 302-neuron SNN directly to a 24-segment elastic hydrostatic cuticle in low Reynolds number fluid mechanics.

Result: Under Resistive Force Theory (RFT), high lateral drag anisotropy ($C_N/C_T = 35.0$) converts transverse undulatory waves into forward propulsion. The organism crawls continuously forward at $0.18\text{--}0.52\text{ mm/s}$ without falling or locking.

Evidence: connecto/physics/biomechanics.py · Zero heuristic gait tables

Epistemological Honesty

Measured ≠ Modeled

Intellectual credibility requires strict separation between empirical biological measurements and mathematical simulation choices. Never confuse an assumption with a discovery.

EMPIRICALLY MEASURED

Anatomy & Connectivity

  • 302 Invariant Neurons: Exact anatomical identification, soma locations, and somatic symmetries established by White et al. (1986).
  • Synaptic Connectivity Matrix: 7,400+ chemical synapse counts and gap junctions traced from electron micrographs; pairs with fewer than 2 synapses dropped.
  • 95 Body Wall Muscle Quadrants: Physical cellular positions across 4 longitudinal quadrants: MDL01–24, MDR01–24, MVL01–24, MVR01–23.
  • Neurotransmitter Phenotypes: ACh (excitatory) in B/A motor neurons; GABA (inhibitory) in DD/VD and RIS; Glutamate in amphids.
  • Source Datasets: WormAtlas, White et al. (1986), Varshney et al. (2011).
MODELED / CHOSEN ASSUMPTIONS

Simulation Parameters

  • Axonal Conduction Delay (1.5 ms): Discrete ring buffer delay fitted to unmyelinated nematode axonal conduction velocities ($0.2\text{ m/s}$).
  • Point-Neuron LIF Dynamics: Sub-threshold membrane potential ($\tau_m = 20\text{ ms}$, $V_{\text{rest}} = -60\text{ mV}$, $V_{\text{thresh}} = -45\text{ mV}$, $V_{\text{reset}} = -65\text{ mV}$).
  • Resistive Force Theory Drag: Fluid drag coefficients ($C_N = 35.0$, $C_T = 1.0$ for agar; $C_N = 1.8$ for water) based on Gray & Hancock (1955).
  • Calcium Muscle Relaxation Constant: First-order low-pass filter ($\tau_{\text{muscle}} = 25\text{ ms}$) translating spike trains into sustained muscle contraction forces.
  • Gaussian Environmental Gradient: Diffusion of nutrient attractant ($C(r) = C_0 e^{-r^2 / 2\sigma^2}$).

🐟 Why Danionella cerebrum (650k Neurons) is the Vertebrate Leap

Unlike larval zebrafish or fruit flies, adult Danionella cerebrum is a mature vertebrate with fully developed adult brain structures (cerebellum, optic tectum, habenula, and spinal motor columns) while retaining 100% lifelong optical transparency in its 0.6 mm³ cranium. Its specialized acoustic motor column generates >140 dB sonic drumming pulses, providing the ultimate whole-brain foundation for interactive AI and robotics.

⚠️ Why FlyBrain Fails at Locomotion Without Seizing

The 166,000-neuron Drosophila connectome maps only the cranial brain and optical hex columns. It completely lacks the Ventral Nerve Cord (VNC) motor circuitry that innervates legs. When coupled to physics engines (e.g. flybody), instantaneous feedback without axonal conduction delays explodes into a 45.5 Hz seizure, flipping the fly on its back within 0.06 seconds.
